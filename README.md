## Stephen Nyaranga

Climate scientist and geophysicist. I am doing a PhD in Climate Science as a CLARITY-Africa Fellow. CLARITY-Africa is coordinated by Imperial College London, and my fellowship is awarded through the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Research and Innovation Centre (AIMS-RIC) in Kigali. I am also registered at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT) for a PhD in GIS and Remote Sensing.

My research combines satellite radar (Sentinel-1 SAR), InSAR and deep learning to map flood extent, estimate flood depth and support urban resilience in Rwanda, with a focus on Kigali and Musanze. More broadly, I am interested in putting satellite technology to work in disaster risk reduction and early warning, agriculture, energy and water resources.
